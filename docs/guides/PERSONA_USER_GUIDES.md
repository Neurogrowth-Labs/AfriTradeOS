# AfriTrade OS Persona User Guides

## Purpose

This guide gives each AfriTrade OS persona a practical, role-specific starting point. Use it with the product documentation for module descriptions and with the workflow diagrams for end-to-end process context.

## Common first steps for all users

1. Sign in or create an account.
2. Complete onboarding by selecting your persona, entering profile details, and adding organization information.
3. Confirm that your default workspace matches your role.
4. Review notifications, currency, language, and theme preferences from the application header.
5. Open **Profile / Settings** to review identity, organization, security, integrations, AI, billing, and audit information.

## SME Exporter

### Primary workspace

- **Dashboard** for KPIs, alerts, quick actions, and navigation.
- **My Trades** for active export workflows.
- **Trade Finance** for tariff calculations and financing applications.
- **Shipments** for logistics visibility.
- **Market Intelligence**, **Find Partners**, **Tenders & RFQ**, and **Contracts** for commercial growth.
- **Trade Compliance** and **KYC Documents** for readiness.
- **AI Assistant** and **Marketing Studio** for assisted workflows.

### Recommended workflow

1. Open **Dashboard** and review alerts and required actions.
2. Create or update a trade in **My Trades**.
3. Validate HS code, route, Incoterm, compliance readiness, and required documents.
4. Use **Market Intelligence** to validate demand and pricing.
5. Search **Find Partners** for buyers, logistics providers, or finance providers.
6. Review tenders in **Tenders & RFQ** and prepare bids where relevant.
7. Submit KYC/KYB documents from **KYC Documents**.
8. Use **Trade Finance** to compare finance options and apply.
9. Track cargo status from **Shipments**.
10. Use **Contracts** to monitor agreement milestones.

### Success checklist

- Profile and company data complete.
- KYC/KYB documents submitted and verified.
- Trade records include origin, destination, value, HS code, Incoterm, and compliance status.
- Finance and logistics actions are linked to the relevant trade.

## Enterprise Exporter

### Primary workspace

- **Command Center** / **Dashboard** for organization-level visibility.
- **Trade Workspace** for export projects and operational stages.
- **Logistics Grid**, **Trade Finance**, **Partner Network**, **Tenders & RFQ**, and **Contracts** for scaled operations.

### Recommended workflow

1. Review portfolio KPIs and alerts from **Command Center**.
2. Create export projects with project number, priority, product, route, documents, and target shipping dates.
3. Assign owners and team members.
4. Monitor compliance score, AI risk score, finance status, and shipment milestones.
5. Maintain partner records for buyers, carriers, finance providers, and regulators.
6. Review market intelligence and tender opportunities by sector and country.
7. Track contract performance and milestone completion.

### Success checklist

- Each export project has owner, timeline, documents, compliance status, and finance/logistics status.
- Operational exceptions are triaged from alerts.
- Management can view progress by project, country, sector, and partner.

## Importer

### Primary workspace

- **Importer Panel**, covering dashboard, orders, shipments, documents, compliance, finance, analytics, and settings.

### Recommended workflow

1. Open **Importer Panel** and review inbound order and shipment KPIs.
2. Create or update import orders with supplier, source/destination country, Incoterm, transport mode, value, HS code, quantity, and containers.
3. Track shipment milestones from receipt through transit, port arrival, customs clearance, and delivery.
4. Upload or verify commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, certificates, and customs files.
5. Monitor compliance status and customs declaration state.
6. Review finance exposure and analytics.

### Success checklist

- Every import order has current journey status and customs status.
- Required documents are attached before customs submission.
- Exceptions are resolved before ETA where possible.

## Customs Authority

### Primary workspace

- **Customs Panel** for dashboard, review queue, traders, declarations, verification, shipments, analytics, and settings.

### Recommended workflow

1. Review declaration volumes, risk distribution, and urgent alerts from **Dashboard**.
2. Open **Review Queue** and triage declarations by severity and submission age.
3. Validate trader identity, TIN, certificates, route, HS code, declared value, duty/VAT, and AfCFTA preference claims.
4. Record reviews, queries, approvals, rejections, escalations, or clearances.
5. Monitor shipments and holds.
6. Export declaration, trader registry, or revenue reports when required.

### Success checklist

- High-risk declarations have documented review notes.
- Cleared declarations include duty/tax and preference decision records.
- Trader and certificate registries remain current.

## Logistics Provider

### Primary workspace

- **Logistics Panel** for dashboard, fleet, shipments, clients, tenders, invoices, document generation, HS code search, AfCFTA calculator, and settings.

### Recommended workflow

1. Review fleet utilization, delayed shipments, pending invoices, border delays, and tenders.
2. Assign vehicles and drivers based on availability, permits, insurance, and health score.
3. Update shipment milestones, border status, and exception notes.
4. Respond to quote requests and tenders.
5. Generate transport documents and invoices.
6. Use HS code search and AfCFTA calculator to support clients.

### Success checklist

- Vehicles and drivers have valid permits, insurance, and maintenance data.
- Active shipments have current location, ETA, and risk status.
- Invoices and documents are linked to the shipment record.

## Bank / Insurer

### Primary workspace

- **Finance Dashboard** for portfolio and pipeline.
- **Finance Applications** for application intake and review.
- **KYC & AML** for due diligence.
- **Risk & Clients** for market and client monitoring.
- **Trade Tools** for insurance, blockchain verification, and AfCFTA checks.
- **Account Settings** for bank users, roles, currencies, integrations, and audit logs.

### Recommended workflow

1. Review exposure, pipeline, risk radar, document status, and application stages.
2. Open finance applications and review company, contact, financing, trade, and document details.
3. Run or review credit scoring, document extraction, and workflow automation outputs.
4. Complete KYC and AML review.
5. Check client risk and market risk indicators.
6. Quote insurance or financing terms where appropriate.
7. Record decisions, conditions, and audit events.

### Success checklist

- Applications have complete supporting documents and risk scores.
- KYC/AML results are documented before approval.
- Portfolio limits and currency exposure are monitored.

## Government Agency

### Primary workspace

- **Command Center** / **Regulator** for live monitoring.
- **Policy & Compliance**, **Trade Agreements**, **Trade Statistics**, **Trade Flows**, **Entity Verification**, and **Business Registry** for oversight.

### Recommended workflow

1. Review national or agency KPIs, risk alerts, and anomaly detection.
2. Monitor policy compliance and enforcement cases.
3. Review trade agreement utilization, tariff schedules, rules of origin, and preference calculations.
4. Analyze trade statistics by sector, country, corridor, and period.
5. Monitor border posts, congestion, and route disruptions.
6. Verify trusted traders and registry records.
7. Export reports for policy and operational review.

### Success checklist

- Policy cases have status, severity, assignment, and resolution notes.
- Agreement and tariff reference data remain current.
- Registry and trusted trader records are reviewed periodically.

## Trade Analyst

### Primary workspace

- **Analytics Hub**, **Market Research**, **Trade Trends**, **Regulatory Data**, **Logistics Data**, **Finance Metrics**, **Market Players**, and **Tender Analysis**.

### Recommended workflow

1. Start from **Analytics Hub** to identify current signals.
2. Use **Market Research** to compare demand, sentiment, pricing, and country opportunities.
3. Review **Trade Trends** for corridor, commodity, or disruption patterns.
4. Review **Regulatory Data** for policy changes or compliance burden.
5. Use **Logistics Data** to assess border, route, and carrier performance.
6. Use **Finance Metrics** to assess credit availability and cost.
7. Review **Market Players** and **Tender Analysis** to identify commercial opportunities.

### Success checklist

- Findings cite module outputs and filters used.
- Recommendations distinguish market opportunity, risk, and operational constraint.
- Exported datasets or reports are stored with date and scope.

## Platform Admin

### Primary workspace

- **Admin Console**, **Oversight Portal**, **System Health**, and all platform modules.

### Recommended workflow

1. Review tenant health, alerts, service status, queues, API keys, plans, and logs.
2. Investigate platform alerts and failed jobs.
3. Manage tenants, roles, and access control.
4. Review audit logs and export evidence when needed.
5. Pause/resume global trade workflows only under approved incident procedures.
6. Coordinate schema, RLS, environment, and integration changes with deployment runbooks.

### Success checklist

- Admin actions are logged and tied to a reason.
- Production-impacting actions have approval and rollback notes.
- Service health and queue status are monitored after changes.
